I am André Nicolet, Professor at Aix-Marseille Université (PREX1, 63rd section: Electronics, Optronics and Systems) and a member of Institut Fresnel (CNRS UMR 7249), within the ATHENA team, which I founded in 2015.

My research lies at the crossroads of computational electromagnetism and photonics: finite-element modeling of microstructured optical fibers, metamaterials, and transformation optics — a framework I introduced in 2003, a few years before it gained wide attention through John Pendry's invisibility cloak. Since then, my work has focused on the modal analysis of open, dispersive photonic structures through quasi-normal modes (QNM), and on open-source tools such as ONELAB Photonics.

Alongside research, I teach electromagnetism and applied mathematics, from undergraduate courses to the Erasmus Mundus M1 Europhotonics programme and the Master MSPP, which I co-founded with colleagues at Université Cheikh Anta Diop (Dakar). I also sit on the advisory board of Ghost Space SRL, a deep-tech startup developing stray-light correction for space-based optics.
